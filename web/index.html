PI-RADS v2.1 combination rules

  • Peripheral zone: DWI determines category; positive DCE upgrades DWI 3 to PI-RADS 4.
  • Transition zone: T2W 2 with DWI 4–5 becomes PI-RADS 3; T2W 3 with DWI 5 becomes PI-RADS 4.
  • Central zone and anterior fibromuscular stroma require specialist interpretation and are not automatically mapped onto transition-zone rules.
